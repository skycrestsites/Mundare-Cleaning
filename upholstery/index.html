About This Service

Give Your Furniture a Second Life.

Sofas, chairs, and upholstered furniture accumulate dirt, dust mites, stains, and odors over time — especially in homes with kids and pets. Our upholstery steam cleaning service uses hot steam extraction to deep-clean every fiber, sanitizing and deodorizing your furniture without harsh chemicals.

Whether your sofa has seen years of use or you just want a thorough refresh before guests arrive, we work to restore your upholstery to its best possible condition. It's a cost-effective alternative to replacing furniture — and the results often speak for themselves.
