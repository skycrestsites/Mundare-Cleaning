About This Service

Carpets That Look — and Smell — New Again.

Over time, carpets accumulate deep-set dirt, stains, and odors that regular vacuuming just can't fix. Our carpet shampoo service uses professional-grade hot water extraction to reach deep into the fibers — lifting out stains, eliminating odors, and restoring the fresh, clean look of your carpet.

We use eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are safe for kids, pets, and the environment — without sacrificing effectiveness. Whether it's a single room or the whole house, we leave every carpet looking and feeling revitalized.

Our Carpet Cleaning Process

1

Pre-Inspection

We identify stain types and high-traffic areas to customize our approach for your carpet.

2

Pre-Treatment

Eco-friendly pre-treatment solution applied to break down stains and loosen deep-set dirt.

3

Hot Water Extraction

Professional steam extraction pulls dirt, stains, and odors from deep within the carpet fibers.

4

Post-Treatment & Dry

Deodorizer applied and carpet left to dry quickly — usually walkable within 4-6 hours.
